# Website: public launch

What changes on the landing page once the app is public on Google Play. The site as built, with the
app in closed testing, is in [website.md](../website.md).

## Google Play button

- The availability setting next to the store address is on, so the Google Play button opens the
  store listing in a new tab and the closed-testing hint is gone.
- The outlined pill is replaced by Google's official Google Play badge, unaltered, as Google's
  brand rules require.
- The badge comes first in the row, ahead of the GitHub button, on both the hero and the closing
  page.

## Closing page

- The line about coming to Google Play and following along on GitHub gives way again to a call to
  join and start learning right now.

## Release

- The launch build is deployed to a preview channel ahead of time, and promoted to the live site
  once the listing is public, so the site flips at the same moment as the store. The commands are in
  [development.md](../../development.md#website).
